# The Paper My State Machine Refused

**Paper:** Yao, Yu, Zhao, Shafran, Griffiths, Cao, Narasimhan (2023). *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models*. arXiv:2305.10601.

*Chapter in progress — the article version is scheduled as the closer.*

Every preceding chapter showed a paper fitting in a page of YAML. This one does not fit.

**Claim under test.** The gap between what the machine expresses — a bounded beam, one ply at a time — and what the paper wants — open-ended search with backtracking — is the sharpest description of what declarative machines are for that this book can give. The closing verdict is about the substrate, not the paper.
