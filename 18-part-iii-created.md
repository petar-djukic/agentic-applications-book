# Part III — Papers, Created

The chapters in Part II document mechanisms that were already running.
These five were not: each paper is landed in the runtime first — merged
and audited — and the chapter then documents what the addition took.

The work is done in the open for a reason. A book that only shows
finished machines teaches the reader that the substrate is complete. It
is not, and the diffs are the honest part: what a new mechanism actually
costs when the architecture already has the right words for it, and what
it costs when the architecture has to grow a new one.

Chapters in this part are gated on the corresponding runtime work
landing. They are listed here as commitments, not as completed material.
